## 4-Layer PIL Thumbnail Architecture (1280×720)

Every thumbnail generated by this factory conforms strictly to a 4-layer composition model:

1. **Layer 1: Base Climax Plate (1280×720 RGB)**
   - Extracted from the video climax block (70%–75% timeline position) or hero concept plate.
   - Shows maximum kinetic scale: volcanic plume, rupture point, shockwave wall, or critical decision point.

2. **Layer 2: Emergency Gradient Overlay**
   - Horizontal left-to-right gradient from `#FF0000` (crimson red) to `#FF6B00` (intense emergency orange).
   - Alpha transparency calibrated to 40% (alpha = 102 / 255) to enhance dramatic contrast while preserving underlying scene geometry.

3. **Layer 3: Upper-Third Bold Title Typography**
   - Positioned in the upper 35% of the frame (safe from YouTube mobile duration badge at bottom-right).
   - High-contrast bold typography: Impact or Montserrat Black (fallback to bold TrueType sans-serif).
   - Color: Pure white `#FFFFFF` with heavy 4px–6px deep black `#000000` drop-shadow / outline for instant legibility against any background.
   - Truncated to 3–5 punchy words maximum.

4. **Layer 4: Lower Anchor Bar & Branding Strip**
   - 48px–64px bottom strip with 60% black opacity (`#000000`, alpha = 153 / 255).
   - Left-aligned channel badge or series icon placeholder (`[FACTORY DNA]`).
   - Bottom-right corner kept clear of text to prevent overlap with YouTube player timecode pill.
