## Oceanographic Standards & Hadal Physics

Every underwater crisis is bounded by the immutable thermodynamics and fluid mechanics of deep water:

1. **Hydrostatic Pressure ($P = \rho g h$):**
   - At the Challenger Deep (10,928 meters), seawater density $\rho \approx 1,028\text{ kg/m}^3$ under extreme compression.
   - Total hydrostatic pressure reaches **1,085 to 1,100 bar (approx. 110 MPa / 16,000 PSI)**.
   - Every square centimeter of hull surface sustains the weight of 1.1 metric tons of water. Pressure acts **isotropically** from every direction simultaneously.

2. **Optical Attenuation & Spectral Absorption:**
   - Water absorbs electromagnetic radiation exponentially ($I(z) = I_0 e^{-\alpha z}$).
   - **Complete Red Absorption at 3 Meters:** Red light ($\lambda = 650\text{--}700\text{ nm}$) is completely absorbed within the first 3 meters of seawater. Blood appears emerald green or pitch black.
   - At depths below 200 meters (mesopelagic), sunlight drops below human visual detection. Below 1,000 meters (bathypelagic and hadal), natural ambient illuminance is exactly **0.000 lux**. Only bioluminescence and artificial projectors exist.

3. **Microsecond Implosion Mechanics:**
   - When a pressurized hull (titanium alloy Grade 5 or thick conical acrylic) suffers structural failure under 1,100 bar, collapse occurs in **1 to 30 milliseconds ($10^{-3}\text{ to }10^{-2}\text{ seconds}$)**.
   - The inward rush of seawater travels faster than the speed of sound in air (~343 m/s).
   - Adiabatic compression of the internal cabin atmosphere heats the enclosed air pocket to over **1,000°C** in a fraction of a millisecond before the violent kinetic hydraulic hammer pulverizes all structures.

4. **Sonar Acoustic Refraction & Shadow Zones:**
   - Sound speed in the ocean is a function of temperature, salinity, and pressure ($c \approx 1449.2 + 4.6T - 0.055T^2 + 0.00029T^3 + (1.34 - 0.010T)(S - 35) + 0.016z$).
   - Thermocline gradients bend acoustic waves, creating acoustic shadow zones where active sonar pings bounce harmlessly away.
   - Benthic hadal trenches act as acoustic absorption traps, deadening echoes against thick organic silt.

## Veo 3.1 Noun Rule & Deepsea Visual Framing

Generative AI video models fail when rendering underwater scenes by introducing cartoon bubbles, blurry human faces, or impossible daylight illumination. Every visual prompt must strictly adhere to the **Veo 3.1 Noun-First Rule**:

1. **Noun-First Architecture:**
   - Open every prompt with tactile materials, viewport geometry, optical clarity, particle suspension, and high-contrast artificial lighting.
   - Example: *"Macro extreme close-up, 200mm thick curved acrylic viewport, delicate branching spiderweb micro-cracks spreading across transparent surface, high-intensity xenon searchlight beam cutting through inky black abyssal seawater, floating white marine snow particles illuminated in beam, hyper-detailed mechanical realism."*

2. **Visual Language & Diegetic Perspectives:**
   - **Spiderweb Viewport Micro-Cracks:** Tension spreading across heavy multi-layer acrylic glass under immense pressure.
   - **Structural Compression & Seam Weeping:** Macro shots of titanium structural ribs coated in condensation droplets, metal seams straining under hydrostatic load. All depth gauges, readouts, and telemetry belong exclusively to `[DATA_BROLL]`.
   - **Illuminated Marine Snow:** Suspended organic biological flecks drifting past the exterior viewport in the narrow cone of an LED projector.
   - **Searchlight Slicing the Void:** A single narrow 10,000-lumen xenon cone cutting through complete, impenetrable hadal darkness to illuminate a sheer basalt trench wall.
   - **Sweating Cold Bulkheads:** Macro shots of titanium structural ribs coated in condensation droplets running down frozen metal surfaces.

3. **Zero Human Face Invariant:**
   - Completely eliminate human facial close-ups. Focus exclusively on gloved hands manipulating heavy bronze valve wheels, reflection of glowing warning diodes in helmet visors, or over-the-shoulder perspectives peering into the abyssal gloom.

4. **Persistent Negative Prompt Guard:**
   - `human faces, smiling, expressive eyes, readable UI text, digital numbers, daylight underwater, tropical fish, cartoon bubbles, smooth CGI textures, watermarks, 2D logos`.

## Mandatory Narrative Invariants

1. **100% English Invariant:** All voiceover narration, audio cues, screen text, and prompts run strictly in English (`en` / `en-US`).
2. **Strictly 2nd-Person Singular POV ("You / Your"):** Every scene opens with "You " or "Your ". The viewer is the lone operator inside the failing pressure vessel.
3. **In-Media-Res Crisis Hook (First 3 Seconds):** Immediate physical peril in Scene 1 (a loud metallic ping as titanium rivets strain, cold water misting from an overhead fitting, the depth gauge ticking deeper).
4. **Clean Narration Gateway:** Spoken prose must be stripped of all bracketed sound directions (`[metal groaning]`, `(breathing heavily)`) and scene headers prior to voice generation.
