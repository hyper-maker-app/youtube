## Infrastructure Standards & Tactical Engineering

Every urban survival scenario adheres to real-world engineering constraints of modern urban infrastructure:

1. **SCADA Automated Grid Failure & Cascading Blackouts:**
   - Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) systems trip automated protective breakers when frequency fluctuates beyond $\pm 0.5\text{ Hz}$ from standard 50/60 Hz.
   - High-voltage transformer cascading disconnect occurs in **under 800 milliseconds**.
   - Municipal municipal water pumps lose pressure within 2 to 4 hours as backup diesel generator fuel lines air-lock or exhaust supplies.
   - High-rise gravity tanks empty within 6 hours, rendering indoor plumbing inoperative.

2. **0 dB Silence Threshold & Sub-15 dB Acoustic Stealth:**
   - In a functioning metropolis, ambient traffic and HVAC noise creates a continuous background of 60 to 75 dB.
   - When the grid collapses, ambient urban sound plummets to **under 20 dB** (near-total silence).
   - In this dead acoustic environment, a dropping tool, metallic gate latch, or crunch of glass produces a 70–80 dB acoustic impulse that carries over 400 meters down canyon streets.
   - Protagonist must maintain **acoustic stealth (< 15 dB movement noise)** by taping loose gear, utilizing felt or rubber sole wraps, and moving exclusively during environmental noise spikes (wind gusts, structural settling).

3. **RF Spectrum Jamming & Squelch Static:**
   - Cellular networks go dark within 2 hours as cell tower backup batteries drain.
   - UHF/VHF emergency bands become clogged with high-voltage arcing electromagnetic interference (EMI) and harsh receiver squelch static.
   - Tactical radio communication must be restricted to frequency-hopping analog burts or line-of-sight laser/optical signalling.

4. **Battery Chemistry & Environmental Drain:**
   - Lithium-ion cells experience internal resistance surges in sub-zero or damp subterranean environments: capacity drops by 30% to 50% under cold loads.
   - Every lumen-hour of tactical illumination is mathematically rationed (*"Torch on low mode: 15 lumens drawing 0.1 watts, 4.2 hours of reserve"*).

## Crisis Blueprints & Tactical Scenarios

1. **Metropolitan Power Grid Total Collapse & High-Rise Descent:**
   - Primary Hazard: Sudden regional blackout traps survivor on the 48th floor of an office tower.
   - Obstacles: Elevators frozen in hoistways, electronic magnetic door locks defaulted to fail-secure or fail-safe depending on fire codes, pitch-black emergency stairwells choked with evacuees.
   - Tactical Action: Utilizing pry bars on mechanical fire dampers, rationing glow sticks, tracking vertical floor markers using low-lumen red LED light.

2. **Subway Transit Tunnel Blackout & Third-Rail Navigation:**
   - Primary Hazard: Commuter subway train stranded 30 meters underground between stations.
   - Obstacles: Complete darkness, standing water puddles with residual capacitor discharge hazard, acoustic amplification of every footstep in tiled tunnels.
   - Tactical Action: Walking strictly along the center wooden ties between rails, avoiding touching the 750V third rail cover board, navigating toward ventilation shaft air currents.

3. **Industrial Chemical Facility Perimeter Breach:**
   - Primary Hazard: Evacuating an automated chemical refinery following cooling pump failure and toxic gas release.
   - Obstacles: Autonomous security sensors, barbed wire perimeter fences, caustic aerosol clouds drifting at ground level.
   - Tactical Action: Utilizing heavy forged-steel bolt cutters on chain-link fences, wearing sealed nitrile-coated tactical gloves and half-face particulate respirators, timing movement with automated camera pan sweeps.

## Veo 3.1 Noun Rule & Urban Shadow Visual Framing

Generative AI video models fail when rendering urban survival scenes by generating generic glossy action heroes, melting firearms, or cartoon explosions. Every visual prompt must strictly adhere to the **Veo 3.1 Noun-First Rule**:

1. **Noun-First Architecture:**
   - Open every prompt with tactile urban hardware, metal alloys, weathered concrete, localized artificial light sources, and low-angle camera framing.
   - Example: *"Macro low-angle shot, heavy forged-steel bolt cutters biting into oxidized chainlink fence padlock, solitary 800-lumen tactical flashlight beam cutting across tiled abandoned subway turnstiles, high-contrast chiaroscuro shadows, gritty urban survival realism."*

2. **Visual Language & Diegetic Perspectives:**
   - **Forged-Steel Bolt Cutters & Hardware:** Macro focus on metal jaws shearing through heavy steel links, sparks showering across cold asphalt.
   - **Flashlight Cutting Pitch-Black Turnstiles:** Solitary narrow beam illuminating dust particles, cracked ceramic tiles, and motionless steel turnstiles in an abandoned subterranean transit station.
   - **Silhouettes Behind Frosted Wire Glass:** Ominous, distorted silhouettes moving in slow motion behind reinforced industrial wire-mesh glass windows.
   - **Crimson Backup Battery LEDs:** Dim, pulsating red LED status diodes blinking in the darkness of a server room or electrical vault, casting an eerie glow on cable bundles.
   - **Dying Skyline Canyons:** Ultra-wide cityscape at twilight where massive skyscraper canyons stand as pitch-black monoliths against a violet, starless sky.

3. **Zero Human Face Invariant:**
   - Completely eliminate human facial close-ups. Frame the survivor exclusively through heavy tactical gloves, floor-level boot tracking shots, silhouetted hooded figures against sodium flare backlighting, or distorted reflections on dark puddle surfaces.

4. **Persistent Negative Prompt Guard:**
   - `human faces, smiling, expressive eyes, readable UI text, digital numbers, daylight city, colorful neon signs, action hero posing, smooth CGI textures, watermarks, 2D printed text`.

## Mandatory Narrative Invariants

1. **100% English Invariant:** All voiceover narration, prompt descriptors, subtitle captions, and audio cues run strictly in English (`en` / `en-US`).
2. **Strictly 2nd-Person Singular POV ("You / Your"):** Every scene opens with "You " or "Your ". The viewer is the tactical survivor navigating the dead metropolis.
3. **In-Media-Res Crisis Hook (First 3 Seconds):** Immediate physical disruption in Scene 1 (the hum of the city dying instantly, magnetic locks snapping shut, elevator plunging into darkness).
4. **Clean Narration Gateway:** Spoken prose must be stripped of all bracketed effects (`[bolt cutters snap]`, `(whispering)`) and scene headers prior to voice generation.
