## Institutional Standards & Astrophysical Frameworks

Every planetary event must be anchored in empirical astrophysics and geophysics:

1. **NASA Center for Near Earth Object Studies (CNEOS):**
   - Hypervelocity kinetic mechanics: Impact velocities range strictly between 11 km/s (Earth escape velocity baseline) and 72 km/s (retrograde hyperbolic cometary entry).
   - Atmospheric displacement: Bolides exceeding 1 km diameter displace the entire atmospheric column in < 2 seconds, creating a vacuum puncture channel allowing ejecta curtain to reach exospheric sub-orbital trajectories.
2. **USGS Volcano Hazards Program & Global Volcanism Project:**
   - Volcanic Explosivity Index (VEI) scaling: VEI-8 defines mega-colossal eruptions with tephra volume $> 1,000\text{ km}^3$ ($> 10^{15}\text{ kg}$).
   - Magma chamber roof collapse, caldera formation, and global plume umbrella dynamics.
3. **NOAA National Tsunami Warning Center (NTWC):**
   - Shallow-water wave equation: Wave propagation speed $c = \sqrt{g \cdot d}$. In deep ocean trenches ($d = 5,000\text{ m}$), $c \approx 800\text{ km/h}$.
   - Shoaling physics: Conservation of wave energy flux causes wave speed to decrease and wave amplitude to amplify vertically by factors of 10x to 30x upon entering shallow coastal shelves.

## Planetary-Scale Metrics & Causal Scaling

1. **1,000 VEI-8 Supervolcanoes Simultaneous Eruption:**
   - Thermal energy release exceeds $10^{22}\text{ Joules}$ (equivalent to millions of megatons of TNT).
   - Plume column heights punch through tropopause into the stratosphere (35–55 km altitude).
   - Ejecta blankets entire continental landmasses under 2 to 5 meters of dense, glassy silicate tephra.

2. **11–72 km/s Asteroid Hypervelocity Impact:**
   - Kinetic energy $E_k = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$. A 10 km chondritic asteroid traveling at 25 km/s delivers $3 \times 10^{23}\text{ J}$ (~70 million megatons).
   - Shock wave creates a transient crater cavity 20x the impactor diameter within 90 seconds.
   - Thermal radiation pulse vaporizes surface structures within a 1,500 km radius before the arrival of the Mach 12 blast wave.

3. **800 km/h Tsunami Shoaling Dynamics:**
   - Open-ocean wave height is deceptive (0.5 to 1.5 meters with a 200 km wavelength).
   - Coastal shoaling: As depth $d$ drops from 4,000 m to 20 m, kinetic energy compresses into a towering 150–300 meter hydraulic wall moving at 60–90 km/h onshore, carrying millions of tons of ocean sediment.

4. **Stratospheric $SO_2$ Aerosol Optical Depth ($AOD > 2.0$):**
   - Injection of $> 500\text{ Mt}$ of sulfur dioxide into the stratosphere forms submicron sulfuric acid aerosol mists ($H_2SO_4$).
   - Aerosol Optical Depth scales beyond 2.0, reflecting 95% of incoming solar irradiance ($< 10\text{ W/m}^2$ reaching surface).
   - Day turns into deep twilight (illuminance $< 2\text{ lux}$).

5. **Volcanic Winter & Hemispheric Temperature Plunge:**
   - Global mean surface temperatures crash along a steep cooling curve: -15°C to -25°C within 120 days.
   - Continental interiors freeze, disrupting the global hydrological cycle and ending rainfall for years.

6. **Magnetosphere Collapse & Satellite Fleet Destruction:**
   - Coronal Mass Ejection (CME) shock or geomagnetic field reversal strips upper ionosphere shielding.
   - Satellite orbital decay from atmospheric thermal expansion; Kessler syndrome debris cloud cascading through Low Earth Orbit (LEO).

## Veo 3.1 Noun Rule & Planetary Visual Framing

Generative AI video models fail when forced to render micro-detailed human faces during planetary cataclysms. Every visual prompt must strictly adhere to the **Veo 3.1 Noun-First Rule**:

1. **Noun-First Architecture:**
   - Open every prompt with macro planetary geography, orbital perspective, atmospheric physics, and lighting temperature.
   - Example: *"Orbital extreme wide-angle view, curvature of planet Earth, incandescent crimson tectonic fracture tearing across continental landmass, dense black stratospheric ash umbrella expanding into space, backlit atmospheric limb glowing electric blue, photorealistic satellite telemetry perspective."*

2. **Visual Language & Diegetic Perspectives:**
   - **Orbital & Stratospheric Vantage:** Curvature of Earth showing continent-scale shockwaves rippling through cloud decks.
   - **Boiling Ocean Horizons:** Distant wall of superheated white vapor rising 15 kilometers into an ash-blackened sky over churning dark seas.
   - **Incandescent Basaltic Rifts:** Glowing red magma fissures carving through snow-covered mountain ranges at twilight.
   - **Extreme High-Altitude Drone Perspectives:** Looking down at a silhouetted survival bunker perched on a barren ridgeline under violet storm clouds.
   - **Cockpit Telemetry & Hardened Viewports:** Scratched quartz glass of an observatory dome, glowing amber analog coordinates, heavy brass pressure seals.

3. **Zero Human Face Invariant:**
   - Strictly prohibit human facial close-ups. Frame protagonists exclusively through silhouette backlighting, full hazardous environment suits with reflective gold-coated visors, or hands operating mechanical override controls.

4. **Persistent Negative Prompt Guard:**
   - `human faces, smiling, expressive eyes, readable UI text, digital clock fonts, 2D printed text, floating logos, watermarks, plastic CGI lighting, cartoon explosion puffs`.
