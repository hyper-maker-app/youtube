## Emergency Standards & Institutional Protocols

Every micro-survival scenario is grounded in institutional disaster protocols:

1. **FEMA P-507 & USAR Field Operations Guide (FOG):**
   - Structural collapse void classification: Pancake collapse, lean-to collapse, V-shape collapse.
   - Acoustic signalling protocol: 3 sharp rhythmic strikes on solid metal pipe or structural concrete every 15 minutes followed by 15 minutes of silent listening.
2. **International Red Cross / Red Crescent & Golden 72 Hours:**
   - 72-Hour Golden Window triage protocol.
   - Hypothermia prevention under darkness: Trapping radiant body heat using emergency mylar thermal blankets and isolating skin from damp concrete slabs.
3. **USGS Earthquake & Liquefaction Hazard Standards:**
   - Aftershock risk modeling: Secondary tremors trigger progressive compaction of collapse voids.
   - Dust inhalation hazards: Fine crystalline silica particulates penetrate pulmonary alveoli without mechanical barrier filtration.

## Micro-Survival Mathematics & Resource Limits

Spectacle without exact physics is unconvincing. Every scene must operate on exact, tangible survival metrics:

1. **11.4L Water Rationing Protocol (72-Hour Survival Kit Baseline):**
   - Exact 11.4 liters (3.0 US gallons) hydration baseline for 72 hours (3.8 liters / 1 gallon per day per person).
   - Under acute void entrapment with zero physical exertion: Minimum survival intake is 250–500 ml per 24 hours, administered in 30 ml sips to prevent vomiting and conserve kidney function.
   - Gulps are strictly prohibited. Narration tracks remaining volume in precise metric units without generic drinking adjectives.

2. **30–60 cm Smoke Evacuation Corridor:**
   - In structural fires, thermal buoyancy forces superheated toxic gases (carbon monoxide $CO$, hydrogen cyanide $HCN$) to the ceiling stratum where temperatures exceed 600°C.
   - The breathable, survivable corridor exists strictly between **30 cm and 60 cm above the finished floor**.
   - Standing erect results in airway thermal flash-burns in under 3 breaths; crawling with nose and mouth in the lowest 45 cm band is mandatory.

3. **P100 & Activated Carbon Particulate Filtration:**
   - Mechanical filtration efficiency: P100 filters capture 99.97% of airborne particulates down to 0.3 microns (pulverized dry-wall, toxic silica dust, radioactive fallout, heavy ash).
   - Activated carbon sorbent beds: Neutralize volatile organic compounds and toxic pyrolysis gases until chemical bed saturation occurs.

4. **Caloric Expenditure & Metabolic Conservation:**
   - Resting metabolic rate in enclosed void: 1,200–1,400 kcal/day.
   - Heavy physical labor (digging through crushed concrete/rebar): 3,200–4,200 kcal/day.
   - Uncontrolled shivering from hypothermia consumes 400 kcal/hour, exhausting glycogen reserves within 6 hours.

## Crisis Blueprints & Physical Mechanics

1. **Sub-Rubble Void Crawl (Post-Seismic Pancake Collapse):**
   - Geometry: 50 cm high crawlspace beneath fractured prestressed concrete slab.
   - Hazards: Jagged #5 rebar protrusions, hanging live conduits, pulverized silica dust obscuring flashlight beams.
   - Physical Action: Tactile navigation using fingertips, clearing debris with minimum arm motion to prevent void shifting, rhythmic acoustic pipe-striking.

2. **Structural Fire & High-Rise Smoke Evacuation:**
   - Geometry: Commercial hallway with zero illumination, dense acrid soot down to 80 cm from floor.
   - Hazards: Thermal door flashover, disoriented navigation in zero visibility.
   - Physical Action: Crawling on all fours with back of gloved hand checking door seams for radiant heat before unlatching.

3. **Flash Flood & Submerged Vehicle Egress:**
   - Geometry: Sedan cabin sinking into 4-meter floodwater.
   - Hazards: Hydrostatic pressure differential prevents door from opening until cabin is 90% flooded and internal/external pressures equalize.
   - Physical Action: Staying calm above rising water pocket, unbuckling harness, preparing spring-loaded center punch against the bottom corner of the tempered side glass window.

## Veo 3.1 Noun Rule & Diegetic Visual Language

AI video generation models fail when instructed with human facial close-ups or abstract emotional adjectives. Every visual prompt must strictly adhere to the **Veo 3.1 Noun-First Rule**:

1. **Noun-First Prompt Architecture:**
   - Always open prompt with concrete physical nouns, camera framing, lighting angle, and material textures before describing action.
   - Example: *"Macro extreme close-up, cracked pre-stressed concrete slab with exposed oxidized steel rebar, dusty tactical flashlight beam illuminating suspended silica particles, high-contrast chiaroscuro lighting, tactile survival aesthetic."*

2. **Atmospheric & Situational Framing (Zero Facial Hallucination):**
   - **Tactile Gear & Tool Macros:** Heavy rescue shears cutting twisted copper wiring, gloved thumb flicking analog lighter wheel, rubberized canteen cap unscrewing.
   - **Diegetic Hardware & Status Diodes:** Pulsating amber LED on a handheld battery pack, emergency strobe light reflections across damp concrete, or sealed battery contact housing. All dials, gauges, needles, or telemetry are routed exclusively to `[DATA_BROLL]`.
   - **Viewports & Peepholes:** Scratched brass door peephole POV, fractured dust-covered respirator visor showing breath fog condensation, narrow gap between concrete rubble looking out at flashing emergency beacons.
   - **Backlit Silhouettes:** Chiaroscuro silhouettes moving through smoke-choked corridors, floor-level boot tracking shots through flooded debris.

3. **Persistent Negative Prompt Guard:**
   - `mirror reflections, readable digital clock text, UI text labels, warped hands with extra fingers, smiling faces, direct facial close-ups, smooth CGI skin, 2D floating text, watermarks`.

## Mandatory Narrative Rules

1. **100% English Invariant:** All voiceover narration, prompt descriptors, subtitle captions, and audio cues run strictly in English (`en` / `en-US`). Zero Turkish text in cinematic outputs.
2. **Strictly 2nd-Person Singular POV ("You / Your"):** Every scene narration opens with "You " or "Your ". The viewer is the lone survivor calculating physical survival odds.
3. **In-Media-Res Crisis Hook (First 3 Seconds):** Immediate physical peril in Scene 1 (air pocket shrinking, door shuddering, water rising past ankles). Zero establishing aerial shots.
4. **Clean Narration Gateway:** Spoken prose must be completely free of scene metadata ("Scene 01:"), bracketed sound effects (`[coughing]`), and parenthetical directions.
